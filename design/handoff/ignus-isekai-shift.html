Prologue

For four days, academic gravity switches off.

IGNUS turns the IIT Jodhpur campus into a magic circle. Its outer berms become the threshold. Step across and the walk to a venue becomes a quest. A courtyard becomes an arena. A lecture hall becomes a stage.

The buildings stay where they are. What changes is the reality inside them.
